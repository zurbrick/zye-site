FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Zye.AI?

Zye.AI is a small, independent AI studio that makes practical tools for individuals who build with AI, not for companies. It currently runs two products: The Build Brief and Unbroke.

What is The Build Brief?

The Build Brief is a free AI brief for people who build with AI, covering the launches, tools and techniques that matter if you ship with AI. You can subscribe at buildbrief.zye.ai and unsubscribe anytime.

What is Unbroke?

Unbroke fixes broken vibe-coded apps. You send your error or repo, agents diagnose it, a human reviews the fix, and you get a plain-English explanation. Details are at unbroke.zye.ai. Pricing and checkout are coming soon.

Are fixes done by AI or by people?

Both. Agents do the heavy lifting, and a person reviews what goes out.

How do I contact Zye.AI?

Email hello@zye.ai with questions, ideas, or a broken app.
