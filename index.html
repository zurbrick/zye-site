FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Zye.AI?

Zye.AI is a small, independent AI studio that makes practical tools for builders and vibe coders. It currently runs two products: The Build Brief and Unbroke.

What is The Build Brief?

The Build Brief is a free daily AI brief for builders, covering the launches, tools and techniques that matter if you ship with AI. You can subscribe at buildbrief.zye.ai and unsubscribe anytime.

What is Unbroke?

Unbroke fixes broken vibe-coded apps. You send your error or repo, agents diagnose it, a human reviews the fix, and you get a plain-English explanation. Details and pricing are at unbroke.zye.ai.

Are fixes done by AI or by people?

Both. Agents do the heavy lifting, and a person reviews what goes out.
